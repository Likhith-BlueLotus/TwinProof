# TwinProof

**Film it. Twin it. Prove it.**

Film a mechanism for 10 seconds; get a calibrated, uncertainty-aware digital twin and a controller that ships with a machine-checked certificate, or an honest refusal naming the failed gate and its number.

The certificate is a model-relative engineering check, not a safety certification.

Status: under construction during the hackathon window. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) and [HACKATHON.md](HACKATHON.md).
